Privacy Policy

Little Linguist  ·  Effective Date: April 11, 2026

Welcome to Little Linguist! Your privacy is incredibly important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our application.

1. Minimal Data Collection

We believe in privacy by design. Little Linguist collects minimal data. Any data we do store — such as your current streaks, longest streaks, and saved favourite phrases — is stored locally on your device (using AppGroup local storage) and is never sent to our servers.

2. Usage of Device Language Settings

Our app relies on your local device language settings to detect your native tongue and provide the core phrase generation functionality seamlessly. No personally identifiable information is derived from this setting.

3. GDPR and CCPA Compliance

In compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • You maintain the right to access, delete, or modify any data stored by this application simply by clearing your local application data or uninstalling the app.
  • We do not process data outside of your device.

4. No Data Sold to Third Parties

We are proud to state that no personal data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties for advertising or any other purposes. You are not the product.

5. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns regarding your privacy while using Little Linguist, please contact us through the Help & Support section within the app.
