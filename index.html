Privacy Policy

Little Linguist  ·  Last updated: 31 October 2026

Little Linguist is made by Liam Todd, an independent developer. This page explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: we don't collect it. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and Little Linguist has no server of its own.

What stays on your device

Your languages and level, saved phrases and notes, streaks, quiz results, the days you opened the app, and your settings are stored on your device (and shared with Little Linguist's widgets).

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, your saved phrases, streaks, quiz history and the days you opened the app are kept in step across your devices using iCloud key-value storage. That copy lives in your own iCloud account, under Apple's privacy policy, and we can't see it. Your settings aren't synced.

Say It Back (microphone and speech recognition)

When you use Say It Back, Little Linguist asks for access to your microphone and to speech recognition so it can hear you say the phrase and check it. Recognition runs on your iPhone whenever your device has the speech model for that language installed. If it doesn't, iOS sends the audio to Apple's speech recognition service to turn it into text, under Apple's privacy policy. Little Linguist never records, stores or sends your voice anywhere else, and nothing is kept after the check.

Listening to phrases

Phrases are read aloud by your iPhone's built-in voices, on the device.

Reminders

The daily phrase reminder is scheduled on your device by iOS. No notification server is involved.

Premium

Premium subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details or Apple ID; Apple only tells the app whether you have Premium.

Your rights

Because we don't hold any of your information, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can remove everything Little Linguist stores on a device by deleting the app.

Children

Little Linguist doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
