Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change my target language?

Tap the Settings tab and select a new language under Target Language. Your streak and favourites are preserved when you switch.

My streak disappeared — what happened?

Streaks are tracked in UTC. If you opened the app near midnight while travelling across time zones, the streak check may have resolved to a different calendar day. Unfortunately streak resets cannot be undone manually.

How do I restore my premium subscription?

Go to Settings → Subscription → Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID used when you originally subscribed.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings → Subscription → Manage on App Store. You can cancel at any time; access continues until the end of the current billing period.

How do I add the widget to my home screen?

Long-press an empty area of your home screen, tap the + button in the top corner, search for Little Linguist, and choose the small or medium widget size.

My favourites are not showing in the widget

The widget reads favourites from shared app storage. Try opening the app once and waiting a moment for the widget to refresh. If the problem persists, removing and re-adding the widget usually resolves it.

The phrase audio is not playing

Check that your device is not on silent and that the volume is turned up. Audio playback uses the system text-to-speech engine — make sure the relevant language voice is downloaded in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.
